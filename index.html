Why the town matters

Fairfax County is not one courthouse.

Herndon and Vienna are incorporated towns inside Fairfax County. Each has its own municipal government and police department. Traffic violations and criminal misdemeanors under their local laws are heard in town courthouses, while Fairfax County General District Court judges sit on those dockets.

That combination—town government, local enforcement, and a county judicial system—is distinctly Virginian. It is also useful context for understanding where a case is actually pending.
