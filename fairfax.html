The courthouse—and the town courts.

Most Fairfax County General District Court cases are heard at the Fairfax County Courthouse. But the Court itself explains that traffic violations and criminal misdemeanors under the local laws of the Town of Herndon and Town of Vienna are heard in courthouses inside those towns. Fairfax County General District Court judges also sit in those town courts, and Fairfax County clerks staff them.

That is why a ticket that says “Herndon” or “Vienna” should not be treated as though the only relevant place is the large courthouse on Chain Bridge Road.

A county with a strange local-government map.

Virginia’s local-government system is unusual. Cities are independent from counties. Towns are different: an incorporated town remains within a county while also having its own government. That means a resident of Herndon or Vienna lives in both a town and Fairfax County at the same time.

For court practice, that overlap becomes concrete. Herndon and Vienna have their own municipal governments and police departments, but Fairfax County General District Court judges hear the town traffic and misdemeanor dockets.
