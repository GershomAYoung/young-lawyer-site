About

Gershom A. Young

Virginia attorney practicing in Fairfax County, with particular attention to traffic and misdemeanor matters in Herndon and Vienna.

Young.Lawyer PC is built around direct, local representation. The firm focuses on understanding the court, the community, and the practical consequences a case may create for the individual client.

Background

Gershom is a graduate of Thomas Jefferson High School for Science and Technology, the University of Virginia, and William & Mary Law School, where Gershom served on the William & Mary Law Review.

Before and during legal training, Gershom's work and interests have included economics, Chinese language and culture, the performing arts, publishing, and community affairs.

Fairfax County

The firm's present focus is local: a general practice in Fairfax County with particular interest in the smaller town dockets and the people whose cases arise there.
