Traffic representation

A traffic case is a bundle of consequences.

The amount printed on a ticket is not necessarily the most important number. A disposition may affect DMV demerit points, the driving record, insurance, licensing, employment, court costs, and time. Which consequences matter depends on the person and the charge.

That is why Young.Lawyer PC does not publish a universal traffic-case price. The initial consultation is free. Fees are determined after learning enough about the matter to understand what representation would require.
